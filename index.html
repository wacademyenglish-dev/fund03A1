VOCABULARY 2/3

Let's talk about our current home.

A building on the ground is a house .

An apartment in a tall building is a flat .

When talking about living inside a place, use: in .

Teacher: "Flat" is British English. In America, they say "Apartment". Does Student live in a house or a flat?

Student Starter "I live in a..."

Chunk 2: Dwellings

Watch and notice the preposition they use to talk about cities!

Did you hear? They say "I live IN London".

VOCABULARY 3/3

Let's talk about the people inside the house.

When sharing your life with people, use: with .

Friends you live with are flatmates .

Parents and siblings are family .

Living alone is living on my own .

Teacher: Do you live with your family, flatmates, or on your own?

Student Starter "I live with my..." "I live on my own."

The Final Pitch

You have 60 seconds to show your skills.

  • Say Hello & Introduce yourself.
  • Say where you are FROM.
  • Say where you LIVE (city + house/flat).
  • Say WHO you live with.
